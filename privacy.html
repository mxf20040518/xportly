Privacy Policy

Xportly · Last updated October 2026

Xportly is a data file viewer for macOS and Windows. Xportly does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

  • Files you open are read only on your computer. Their contents never leave your device.
  • Xportly has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Xportly does not connect to the internet. Links in the Help menu open in your web browser only when you click them.
  • Xportly stores a few settings on your computer in its own app storage — such as the last folder you browsed and the folders you gave it permission to read. Deleting the app removes them.
