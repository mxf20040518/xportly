Xportly

XPT & data viewer for Mac and Windows

Xportly is a fast, native viewer for Mac and Windows for SAS® transport (XPT) and SAS7BDAT datasets, CSV, Excel and Parquet files — no SAS installation needed.

Frequently asked questions

Which files can Xportly open?
SAS transport files (XPORT Version 5/6 and 8/9, .xpt), SAS7BDAT datasets (including compressed files), CSV/TSV, Excel and OpenDocument spreadsheets (.xlsx .xlsm .xlsb .xls .ods) and Apache Parquet.

Why does the Files panel ask for permission to read a folder?
On the Mac, App Store apps can only read folders you choose. Click Grant access… or Folder… and pick the folder once; Xportly remembers it.

Text looks garbled.
Choose the right character encoding in View ▸ Character encoding (UTF-8 or Windows-1252).

Dates show as numbers.
Make sure View ▸ Apply SAS formats is on. Values without a date format are shown as stored.
